# Dimension prompt: agency

On whose behalf does this idea act — its own, or someone else's?

Possible values: **principal / representative / delegate / proxy / instrument.**

Substitution test: take something that acts for itself and make it act for another (a buyer becomes a broker, a user becomes an admin-impersonating-a-user). Take something that acts for another and strip the other away. The substitution splits one actor into two roles — who does the act and who the act is for — and each role usually wants its own name.

Distinct from autonomy (who starts the action) and animacy (whether the actor is alive): a fully autonomous process can still act entirely on someone else's behalf. Naming contrast: **Principal vs. Delegate**, **Sender vs. OnBehalfOf**, **Author vs. Ghostwriter**.
