# Dimension prompt: authority

Is this the canonical source of truth, or a derivative copy of truth that actually lives elsewhere?

Possible values: **authoritative / cached-but-reconciled / mirrored / stale-prone / purely derivative.**

Substitution test: take an authoritative record and ask what its mirrored form would be — who wins when the mirror and source disagree? Take a mirrored record and ask if it would even need the system it's mirroring. Distinct from autonomy (who's allowed to change it) — authority is about whose value counts as correct.
