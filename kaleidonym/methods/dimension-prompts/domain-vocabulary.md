# Dimension prompt: domain-vocabulary

Do the people who use or pay for this already have a word for it?

Possible values: **established term of art (legal, HR, accounting, etc.) / customer's own word / team jargon / engineering-only coinage / no word yet.**

Substitution test: read the name aloud to the domain expert or the customer and ask what they think it is. If they already say something else, the code name will be translated in every conversation forever; if their word is ambiguous or wrong, the coinage has to beat it clearly enough to justify the translation. Check product docs, locale copy, and support tickets before assuming the field has no word.
